The atelier

A season
takes a year.

Each perfume is composed in the season it describes. Rosée was written during one spring, Givre through one long winter.

Materials are bought by harvest, not by catalogue: Florentine orris, Calabrian bergamot, Haitian vetiver, Omani frankincense.

The four share one bottle. Only the glass tint, the liquid and one lobe of the cap change.

01
Harvest

Before ten

Flowers are picked at dawn, while their scent is strongest and the heat has not yet flattened it. Citrus is pressed within a day of picking.

When05:30 — 10:00
WhereGrasse, Calabria
02
Extraction

Distil & press

Neroli is steam-distilled, bergamot cold-pressed from the peel, orris butter drawn from rhizomes that have dried for three years.

Orris drying3 years
MethodSteam, CO₂, press
03
Composition

The formula

Written across a single season, at the times of day the perfume describes. Each trial is worn outdoors before it is judged.

Materials40 — 60
Written overOne season
04
Maceration

Eight weeks

The concentrate rests in steel, in the dark, at a steady cool temperature, until the materials stop arguing with each other. Then it is chilled and filtered.

Rest8 weeks
Held at12 °C
05
Bottling

By hand

Filled, crimped and capped by hand. The batch and bottle number are written on the base so you can always refill the same harvest.

Batch size1,200
RefillsFor life

02

A screw neck instead of a crimp, so the bottle can be refilled at home.

The label

04

The glass

A heavy base and softened corners. Clear glass, tinted only by what is inside.
